How readers differ in judging melanoma

Each dot is one lesion image, positioned by its scores on the first 9 axes along which readers' feature reliance differs: either a t-SNE map of all of them (images close together are treated alike by readers' individual differences) or any two axes directly. Pick a reader to see the probability that they would call each image melanoma.

Readers are ordered by their position on axis 1 (the main dimension of individual differences).
Diamonds are readers, placed by their own positions on the two axes (on their own scale; readers' coordinates are smaller than image scores). Hover for details; click to select that reader.
Lines lesions up from the − end to the + end of the axis. Click an image to find it on the map.
